SHARE2me — Delete Your Data

Last updated: May 2026

SHARE2me stores all data locally on your device. We do not operate any servers and do not store any of your data externally. You have full control over all your data at all times.

How to Delete Your Data

1. Delete Downloaded Media

  1. Open SHARE2me
  2. In the gallery, long-press to select the media you want to delete (or use "Select All")
  3. Tap the delete button to permanently remove selected files

2. Clear Download History

  1. Open SHARE2me
  2. Tap the account icon (top right)
  3. Tap "Clear History" to remove all download history records

3. Remove Instagram/Threads Login Session

  1. Open SHARE2me
  2. Tap the account icon (top right)
  3. Tap "Logout" for Instagram and/or Threads to clear your login session cookies

4. Delete All Tags and Preferences

  1. Go to your device's Settings → Apps → SHARE2me → Storage
  2. Tap "Clear Data" to remove all tags, preferences, and app settings

5. Delete All App Data Completely

  1. Uninstall SHARE2me from your device
  2. This removes all app data, downloaded media stored in the app's internal directory, login sessions, tags, preferences, and history

What Data Is Stored and Deleted

Data Type Stored Where How to Delete
Downloaded media Device only Delete in gallery or uninstall
Download history Device only Clear History or uninstall
Login session cookies Device only Logout or uninstall
Tags & preferences Device only Clear Data or uninstall
Ad identifiers Google AdMob servers Google Privacy Settings
No server-side data to delete: Since SHARE2me does not collect or store any data on external servers, uninstalling the app completely removes all data associated with SHARE2me. The only external data is advertising identifiers managed by Google AdMob, which can be managed through your Google account privacy settings.
