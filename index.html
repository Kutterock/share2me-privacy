SHARE2me Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers and do not collect or transmit any personal data to third parties.

2. Login Information

Instagram and Threads login cookies are stored locally on your device for session access only. We do not have access to your login credentials or passwords.

3. Downloaded Media

All downloaded media is saved to your device storage (internal app directory and Downloads/SHARE2me/). Media files remain under your control.

4. Cloud Backup

If enabled, cloud backup uses your own cloud storage account (Google Drive, OneDrive, etc.). We do not access, read, or store your cloud data on any external server.

5. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's privacy policy. No other analytics or tracking services are used.

6. In-App Purchases

SHARE2me offers an optional Pro subscription through Google Play Billing. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

7. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions necessary for its functionality: storage access for saving media, internet access for downloading, and notification access for download progress.

8. Third-Party Services

This app interacts with Instagram and Threads to retrieve publicly available or user-authorized content. We are not affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc.

9. Children's Privacy

SHARE2me is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and in the app.

11. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the app's Google Play listing page.
